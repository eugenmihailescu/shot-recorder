Shortcuts

Change these at brave://extensions/shortcuts.

  • Visible part: Alt+Shift+V
  • Full page: Alt+Shift+F
  • Selected area: Alt+Shift+A
  • Record this tab: Alt+Shift+R

Visible part after a delay, entire screen, and record desktop have no default key.

Open brave://extensions/shortcuts from the address bar.
